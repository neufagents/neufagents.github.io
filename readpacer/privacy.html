ReadPacer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

ReadPacer is a browser extension that bolds the first letters of words on the web pages you visit, to support focused reading.

Short version: ReadPacer collects no data at all. Nothing you read, type, or browse ever leaves your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. ReadPacer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, page content, or usage analytics.

What stays on your device

Your settings (on/off switch and bold strength) are saved locally in your browser using the extension storage API. They never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage - saves your settings locally on your device.
  • activeTab - lets the popup communicate with the current tab when you use it.
  • Access to web pages - required so the extension can apply the reading format to page text. All text processing happens locally in your browser; no page content is read, stored, or sent anywhere.

Third parties

None. There are no analytics services, no ad networks, and no external servers involved in the operation of ReadPacer.
